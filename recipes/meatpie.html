Meat Pie

Home

Description

Nigerian Meat pie is one of Nigeria’s popular snack and it’s loved by all. The buttery shortcrust pastry is filled with mincemeat cooked in rich flavour. It is delicious and you won't be able to stop at one. Do you want to learn how to make a perfect meat pie? this recipe is for you.

Ingredients

  • All purpose flour
  • Butter
  • Carrots
  • Minced beef
  • Potatoes

Steps

  1. Make the dough: Combine flour, baking powder, and a pinch of salt in a bowl. Rub in cold margarine or butter until crumbly, then slowly add cold water until a smooth dough forms. Wrap and chill for 30 minutes.
  2. Cook the filling: Sauté chopped onions in oil, add minced beef and cook until browned. Stir in diced potatoes, carrots, seasoning cubes, thyme, and salt, then add a splash of water to simmer until soft. Thicken with a flour-water paste and let cool.
  3. Roll and cut: Roll out the dough on a floured surface to about 5mm thickness. Use a circular cutter (or a meat pie mold) to cut out circles.
  4. Fill and seal: Place a spoonful of the cooled meat filling in the center of each circle. Fold the dough over into a half-moon shape and press the edges firmly with a fork to seal.
  5. Bake: Arrange the pies on a lined baking tray and brush the tops with egg wash (beaten egg). Bake in a preheated oven at 170°C (335°F) for 30–40 minutes until golden brown.
