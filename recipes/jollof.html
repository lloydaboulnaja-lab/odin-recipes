Description

A dish that requires simple steps, but is seemingly complicated. To some people, it comes naturally whilst for others it’s a journey to master just the basics. However, I say it’s a journey that is worth it all! A delicious dish that encompasses: Blitz, fry, simmer, and steam, no more, no less. This recipe will guide even the novice of novices to embark on this delicious journey. One of the many Jollof rice variations that I promise you will enjoy.

The ingredients for jollof rice each play a crucial role in creating its rich, vibrant flavour. Tomatoes, red bell pepper, and red onion form the base of the dish, providing sweetness, acidity, and a depth of flavour. Fresh ginger and garlic add aromatic warmth and a subtle spice that enhances the overall taste
