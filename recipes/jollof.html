Description

A dish that requires simple steps, but is seemingly complicated. To some people, it comes naturally whilst for others it’s a journey to master just the basics. However, I say it’s a journey that is worth it all! A delicious dish that encompasses: Blitz, fry, simmer, and steam, no more, no less. This recipe will guide even the novice of novices to embark on this delicious journey. One of the many Jollof rice variations that I promise you will enjoy.

The ingredients for jollof rice each play a crucial role in creating its rich, vibrant flavour. Tomatoes, red bell pepper, and red onion form the base of the dish, providing sweetness, acidity, and a depth of flavour. Fresh ginger and garlic add aromatic warmth and a subtle spice that enhances the overall taste

Ingredients

  • Long grain rice
  • Tomatoes
  • Pepper
  • Tomato paste
  • Chicken or beef stock

Steps

  1. Blend the pepper base: Blend together red bell peppers, tomatoes, Scotch bonnet peppers (atarodo), and onions until completely smooth.
  2. Fry the tomato paste: Heat vegetable oil in a large pot, fry sliced onions until translucent, then add tomato paste and fry for about 5 minutes, stirring constantly to remove the sourness.
  3. Cook the sauce: Pour the blended pepper mix into the pot. Season with curry powder, thyme, bay leaves, white pepper, salt, and bouillon cubes, then fry until the oil separates to the top.
  4. Add rice and stock: Wash long-grain parboiled rice thoroughly with warm water. Stir the rice into the fried sauce, then pour in beef or chicken stock so it sits just barely above the level of the rice.
  5. Steam to finish: Cover the pot tightly with aluminum foil and then the lid to trap the steam. Cook on low heat for 20–30 minutes until the rice is tender, allowing a slight burn at the bottom for that classic smoky Jollof flavor.
