Ice Cream Recipe

Home

Description

Ice cream is a popular frozen dessert made from cream or butterfat, milk, sugar, and flavorings. Some kinds, such as frozen custard and French-style ice cream, also have eggs.

Commercial ice cream is made by mixing liquid ingredients like milk, cream, and syrups with dry ingredients like sugar, stabilizers, and dried eggs or milk. This mix is pasteurized, homogenized, ripened in a refrigerated vat, and then combined with chopped fruit or nuts. While freezing, the mix is agitated to incorporate air and control ice crystal size. Finally, the partially frozen ice cream is packaged and hardened.

Ingredients

  • Whole milk
  • Salt
  • Vanilla extract
  • Heavy cream
  • Sugar

Steps

  1. Prep the cream: Pour cold heavy whipping cream into a large, chilled bowl.
  2. Whip to stiff peaks: Use a hand mixer or stand mixer to whip the cream on high speed until stiff peaks form.
  3. Mix the base: Gently fold sweetened condensed milk and vanilla extract into the whipped cream using a spatula until fully combined.
  4. Add flavors (Optional): Fold in any desired mix-ins like crushed cookies, chocolate chips, or fruit purees.
  5. Freeze: Pour the mixture into a loaf pan or airtight container, cover tightly, and freeze for at least 6 hours or overnight until firm.
